## Numerical meaning

Transport rows have nonnegative mass at most one. A request can attenuate edges before all graph consumers. The double-field equation is `u = (1-alpha)S + alpha Pᵀu`, with `S` normalized from Sense energy. Each finite solve reports the returned-point residual `r` and error bound `r / (1-alpha * max_row_mass)`. Basin projection can omit a direction while preserving scalar field values.

River has one query-level Ω and computes candidate evidence for each ordered path. It selects the path after applying the shared topology budget and ranks by the resulting total. Ordinary and cross-source transport contribute their maximum under that budget. The source ledger apportions derived support over declared roots, uses the maximum contribution per root and discounts candidate self-support. Unknown-origin support stays in total edge support and has no confirmed root.

Independent DTSC field sampling keeps exact concept matches even when interpolated neighbors are limited. DTSC curve evidence uses the selected ordered path, and its trust decision is based on field and candidate-pool statistics. Auxiliary geometry and identity floors are individually configurable.
